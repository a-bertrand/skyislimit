Crisp pixel blocks, absolutely no smooth gradients or fine detail. This is the tile itself, not a preview of multiple repetitions.

Friendly readable game monster rather than horror. Style: genuinely LOW RESOLUTION pixel art designed on a logical 32 by 32 pixel grid, enlarged using crisp uniformly sized square pixels; limited 10-color palette, no antialiasing or gradients. Entire beetle centered, all legs and silhouette inside the image, modest transparent margin around it. Single static pose, not an animation sheet, no repeated views, no text, no labels, no border, no floor, no background, no shadow. Actual transparent background. At final native 32x32 resolution it must remain easily recognizable.
